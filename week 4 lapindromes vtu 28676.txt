T = int(input())

for _ in range(T):
    s = input().strip()
    n = len(s)

    left = s[:n // 2]
    right = s[(n + 1) // 2:]

    if sorted(left) == sorted(right):
        print("YES")
    else:
        print("NO")   




Sample Input
6
gaga
abcde
rotor
xyzxy
abbaab
ababc

YES
NO
